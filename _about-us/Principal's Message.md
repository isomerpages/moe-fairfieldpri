<p>Dear Parents and Guardians,</p>
<p>As we step into Term 4, I reflect on what has been a deeply meaningful
Term 3, one filled with moments of joy, generosity, and community.</p>
<p>We celebrated our 138th Founder's Day through Sophia Blackmore Oaks (OAKS
— Our Acts of Kindness), where our students and families donated daily
necessities and packed them into care packs for our non-teaching support
staff and beneficiaries from the Care Community Service Society (CCSS).
It was heartwarming to see our students giving generously, honouring the
legacy of Miss Sophia Blackmore. Thank you for encouraging your children
to participate and role-modelling generosity at home.</p>
<p><strong>Celebrating Our Teachers</strong>
<br>In that same spirit of gratitude, I want to speak about our teachers as
we have just celebrated Teachers' Day.</p>
<p>Most of what our teachers do happens quietly and out of sight. Beyond
preparing and delivering lessons, marking and administrative work, they
keep a watchful eye over children during recess and dismissal, look carefully
and fairly into incidents between students and sit patiently with a child
who is upset or struggling. They are at once educators, caregivers, counsellors
and mentors, carrying all these roles with dedication and genuine love
for the children in their care.</p>
<p>I know that many of our parents already appreciate and echo these sentiments,
thank you sincerely for that. Please continue to work alongside our teachers
as partners so that every child at Fairfield enjoys a positive and enriching
learning experience.</p>
<p>A special thank you to our PiE Committee, who not only organise parent
volunteers to support school programmes throughout the year, but also put
in tremendous heart to organise a wonderful alfresco breakfast for all
our staff on Tuesday 1 September to celebrate Teachers' Day. It was a gesture
that meant a lot to our teachers and speaks volumes about the warmth of
our Fairfield community.</p>
<p><strong>National Recognition for Two of Our Teachers</strong>
<br>We are proud to celebrate two of our teachers recognised at the national
level this term. Miss Chan Sew Yoon (Assistant Year Head) has received
the Caring Teacher Award (National Level), recognising teachers who show
genuine and holistic care for their students and Mrs Jacelyn Kwan (Year
Head) received the LEAP Award (National Level), recognising outstanding
dedication to professional growth and making a meaningful difference in
students' lives. Both awards involved many nominations and a rigorous selection
process, and it is a tremendous honour for our school.</p>
<p>These two awards are not just a reflection of two individuals, they are
a reflection of the deep care and heartfelt work that all our teachers
pour in every single day. We celebrate these wins as a whole school, knowing
that they are a reflection of the dedication and heart that Fairfield teachers
bring to their work.</p>
<p><strong>A Word for Our P6 Families</strong>
<br>Finally, a special word for our Primary 6 families. PSLE Listening Comprehension
and Written Exams will take place in the coming term. I know you will walk
this journey with your child, not ahead of them. We will work together
to give them confidence, avoid comparisons and will not let our anxiety
find its way to them. What they need most is a calm and loving environment
where they can do their revision. When we walk alongside our children with
encouragement and belief in them, we equip them to be strong and courageous
as they sit down to take each written paper. Let’s encourage them to do
their best and remind them that their best is always enough.</p>
<p>Thank you for your continued partnership and trust. Together, let us make
Term 4 a season of courage, care, and celebration.</p>
